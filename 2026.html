EDS research 2026

Every entry here is research about Ehlers-Danlos syndrome (EDS), or about a related condition as it relates to EDS. This is research information only, not medical advice.

Ehlers-Danlos syndrome research is the index of years. This page covers 2026 only.

November 2026

Ehlers-Danlos syndrome

  • "Expanding neurologic phenotypes in Ehlers-Danlos syndromes: a narrative review." Frontiers in Neurology, 2026 Nov 06. Narrative review of 56 publications covering autonomic dysfunction, small fiber neuropathy, and structural neurologic complications including craniocervical instability; CCI diagnosis in EDS remains controversial, with proposed thresholds (clivo-axial angle under 135 degrees, Harris over 12 mm, Grabb-Oakes over 9 mm) not universally validated.[1]
  • "Clinical characteristics of postural tachycardia syndrome with and without hypermobile Ehlers-Danlos syndrome: a pilot study." Hirosawa H, Nakane S, et al., Frontiers in Neurology, 2026 Nov 03. Retrospective pilot of 21 patients with POTS, 4 of whom also had hEDS; small sample, hypothesis-generating.[2]
  • "Zevra Therapeutics Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Corporate Update." Zevra Therapeutics, 2026-11-03. Company announcement, not a peer-reviewed journal article. The Phase 3 DiSCOVER trial of celiprolol for vascular EDS enrolled six patients in Q3 2026, bringing total enrollment to 72.[3]
  • "More common than we thought: Hypermobile Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome, comorbidity burden, and long COVID risk in a national cohort." Pearson ML, Laraway BJ, et al., PLOS Digital Health, 2026 Nov; 5(11):e0001707. PMID 42986813.[4]
  • "Prevalence of generalized joint hypermobility, hypermobility spectrum disorders and Ehlers-Danlos syndrome: systematic review and meta-analysis." Carnerup I, Bondesson E, et al., Rheumatology International, 2026 Nov 05; 46(11). PMID 42986321. Systematic review and meta-analysis of 17 studies.[5]
  • "Early-onset severe dilated cardiomyopathy in a child with COL5A1 and ABCC9 variants: a possible complex genetic contribution." Cardiology in the Young, 2026 Nov 06, online ahead of print. Single case report.[6]
  • "Sex-associated territory-specific vascular phenotypes in genetically confirmed vascular Ehlers-Danlos syndrome." Abdul Nabi H, Dreher LA, et al., Open Heart, 2026 Nov 02; 13(2).[7]
  • "Hypermobile Ehlers-Danlos syndrome with an intracranial aneurysm." Zou Y, Zhang T, et al., QJM, 2026 Nov 02, online ahead of print. Single case report.[8]
  • "When Connective Tissue Becomes Neurological: A Perspective on Structural Vulnerability, Dynamic Pathology, and the Nervous System." Journal of Personalized Medicine, 2026;16(11):559, published 2026-10-30. A perspective on connective tissue, the nervous system, and structural vulnerability; it critically appraises CCI imaging and surgical series.[9]
  • "Interpreting the time course of lidocaine effectiveness in Ehlers-Danlos syndrome." AlShtait SS, Regional Anesthesia and Pain Medicine, published online 2026-10-23, online ahead of print. Letter to the editor, not original research.[10]
  • "Femoral Artery Access Outcomes and Technical Considerations in Vascular Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome." Lian T, Davis A, Griffith G, Minoza K, Kaufman C, Shalhub S, Journal of Vascular Surgery, published online 2026-10-27, online ahead of print.[11]
  • "Unraveling Gastrointestinal Symptoms in Hypermobile Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome." Levin R, Gazit Y, Carter D, Journal of Neurogastroenterology and Motility, 2026;32(4):625-633.[12]
  • "Pain, but not hypermobility, is associated with borderline personality disorder symptoms among individuals diagnosed with EDS or G-HSD." Williams CM et al., Journal of Clinical Psychology in Medical Settings, 2026 Oct 30, online ahead of print.[13]
  • "Joint Hypermobility and Female Lower Urinary Tract Symptoms: A Systematic Review." Kuroda K, Ito K, Neurourology and Urodynamics, first published 2026-10-26, online ahead of print. Systematic review of 15 studies.[14]

October 2026

Ehlers-Danlos syndrome

  • "Novellia Research Finds Health Systems Typically Miss a Third of an Ehlers-Danlos Patient's Medical History, Complicating Diagnosis and Research." Novellia, 2026-10-26. Company research announcement, presented as Poster #IR026 at the NORD Rare Diseases and Orphan Products Breakthrough Summit; not a peer-reviewed journal article.[15]
  • "Physician Perspectives Surrounding Hypermobile Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome." Cureus, 2026-10-27.[16]
  • "Late Recognition of Suspected Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome After Recurrent Small-Bowel Perforations: A Case Report." Horiguchi N et al., Journal of General and Family Medicine. Case report.[17]
  • "Researchers Link Fragile X Premutation to EDS Phenotype." Wisevoter, 2026-10-23. Secondary report of a pilot study; no journal or primary study citation was given, so this is logged here but not presented as confirmed research.[18]
  • "Defining the chronic complexities of hEDS and HSD." Daylor V et al., Journal of Clinical Medicine, 2025;14(16):5636.[19]
  • "KLK15 alters connective tissues in hypermobile Ehlers-Danlos syndrome." Gensemer C et al., iScience, 2025. KLK15 identified as a novel disease gene candidate in hypermobile EDS. Also listed in the check log as the ScienceDirect KLK15 item; same paper.[20]
  • "Proteomic discoveries in hypermobile Ehlers-Danlos syndrome." Griggs M et al., ImmunoHorizons, 2025.[21]
  • "Label-free differentiation of classical and hypermobile Ehlers-Danlos syndromes using Mueller matrix polarimetry." Tumanova K et al., Biophotonics Discovery, 2026;3(1):015002. Mueller matrix polarimetry distinguishing classical and hypermobile EDS.[22]
  • "Association between Ehlers-Danlos syndrome and complex regional pain syndrome: a large nationwide claims database study." Ren R et al., Regional Anesthesia and Pain Medicine, online first 2026-07-03. Large nationwide claims database study.[23]
  • "Exploring cross-category relationships between symptoms in people with hypermobile EDS (hEDS) to identify disability patterns." Andrews J et al., PLOS ONE, 2026;21(9).[24]
  • "Obstructive sleep apnea and CPAP efficacy in patients with hypermobile Ehlers-Danlos syndrome and hypermobility spectrum disorder." Zhang KL et al., Sleep and Breathing, 2026;30(4):212. Case-control study of obstructive sleep apnea and CPAP efficacy in hEDS and HSD.[25]
  • "Lower androgen sulfate metabolites in women with hypermobile Ehlers-Danlos syndrome." Hannon SL et al., Journal of Steroid Biochemistry and Molecular Biology, 2026;264:107090.[26]
  • "Anesthesia Clinical Practice Guidelines for Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome: A Modified Delphi Survey." Amaral D et al., AANA Journal, 2026;94(5):351-358. First consensus-based anesthesia practice guidelines for EDS.[27]
  • "A Major Update Is Coming to EDS and HSD Diagnosis." The Ehlers-Danlos Society, 2026. Updated EDS/HSD diagnostic criteria to be published in the American Journal of Medical Genetics in December 2026, with care and management pathways in March 2027.[28]
  • "New Model of Care for EDS and HSD." The Ehlers-Danlos Society, 2026. Model of Care work tied to Road to 2026.[29]
  • "Research Roundup: New Research in EDS and HSD." The Ehlers-Danlos Society. A roundup covering several studies, not a single article.[30]

Cranial cervical instability

  • "Evaluating the role of craniocervical and atlantoaxial instability in autonomic dysfunction: a narrative review." Frontiers in Neuroscience, published 2026-10-26. Narrative review of 43 studies.[31]
  • "Craniocervical instability after inadvertent neck hyperextension in Ehlers-Danlos syndrome: a retrospective case series and literature review." PubMed PMID 41872801. Eight hEDS patients developing CCI symptoms after procedures involving neck hyperextension.[32]
  • "Craniocervical instability in patients with Ehlers-Danlos syndromes: outcomes analysis following occipito-cervical fusion." PubMed PMID 38163828. Retrospective outcomes analysis of 53 EDS patients undergoing occipito-cervical fusion.[33]
  • "Craniocervical Instability in Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome, A Systematic Review of Diagnostic and Surgical Treatment Criteria." PMC9609512. Systematic review.[34]
  • "Cervicogenic Dizziness Associated With Craniocervical Instability: A Case Report." Journal of Medical Cases. Case report linking cervicogenic dizziness to CCI and clivo-axial angle correction.[35]
