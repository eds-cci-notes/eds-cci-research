EDS research 2026

This is research information only, not medical advice.

Every entry here is research about Ehlers-Danlos syndrome (EDS), or about a related condition as it relates to EDS. This is research information only, not medical advice.

Ehlers-Danlos syndrome research is the index of years. This page covers 2026 only.

November 2026

Ehlers-Danlos syndrome
  • "Expanding neurologic phenotypes in Ehlers-Danlos syndromes: a narrative review." Narrative review of 56 publications covering autonomic dysfunction, small fiber neuropathy, and structural neurologic complications including craniocervical instability; CCI diagnosis in EDS remains controversial, with proposed thresholds (clivo-axial angle under 135 degrees, Harris over 12 mm, Grabb-Oakes over 9 mm) not universally validated.[1]
  • "Clinical characteristics of postural tachycardia syndrome with and without hypermobile Ehlers-Danlos syndrome: a pilot study." Retrospective pilot of 21 patients with POTS, 4 of whom also had hEDS; small sample, hypothesis-generating.[2]
  • "Zevra Therapeutics Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Corporate Update." Company announcement, not a peer-reviewed journal article. The Phase 3 DiSCOVER trial of celiprolol for vascular EDS enrolled six patients in Q3 2026, bringing total enrollment to 72.[3]
  • "More common than we thought: Hypermobile Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome, comorbidity burden, and long COVID risk in a national cohort."[4]
  • "Prevalence of generalized joint hypermobility, hypermobility spectrum disorders and Ehlers-Danlos syndrome: systematic review and meta-analysis." Systematic review and meta-analysis of 17 studies.[5]
  • "Early-onset severe dilated cardiomyopathy in a child with COL5A1 and ABCC9 variants: a possible complex genetic contribution." Single case report.[6]
  • "Sex-associated territory-specific vascular phenotypes in genetically confirmed vascular Ehlers-Danlos syndrome."[7]
  • "Hypermobile Ehlers-Danlos syndrome with an intracranial aneurysm." Single case report.[8]
  • "When Connective Tissue Becomes Neurological: A Perspective on Structural Vulnerability, Dynamic Pathology, and the Nervous System." A perspective on connective tissue, the nervous system, and structural vulnerability; it critically appraises CCI imaging and surgical series.[9]
  • "Interpreting the time course of lidocaine effectiveness in Ehlers-Danlos syndrome." Letter to the editor, not original research.[10]
  • "Femoral Artery Access Outcomes and Technical Considerations in Vascular Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome."[11]
  • "Unraveling Gastrointestinal Symptoms in Hypermobile Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome."[12]
  • "Pain, but not hypermobility, is associated with borderline personality disorder symptoms among individuals diagnosed with EDS or G-HSD."[13]
  • "Joint Hypermobility and Female Lower Urinary Tract Symptoms: A Systematic Review." Systematic review of 15 studies.[14]

Cranial cervical instability

No new CCI items were noted in November 2026.

October 2026

Ehlers-Danlos syndrome
  • "Novellia Research Finds Health Systems Typically Miss a Third of an Ehlers-Danlos Patient's Medical History, Complicating Diagnosis and Research." Company research announcement, presented as Poster #IR026 at the NORD Rare Diseases and Orphan Products Breakthrough Summit; not a peer-reviewed journal article.[15]
  • "Physician Perspectives Surrounding Hypermobile Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome."[16]
  • "Late Recognition of Suspected Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome After Recurrent Small-Bowel Perforations: A Case Report." Case report.[17]
  • "Researchers Link Fragile X Premutation to EDS Phenotype." Secondary report of a pilot study; no journal or primary study citation was given, so this is logged here but not presented as confirmed research.[18]
  • "Defining the chronic complexities of hEDS and HSD."[19]
  • "KLK15 alters connective tissues in hypermobile Ehlers-Danlos syndrome." KLK15 identified as a novel disease gene candidate in hypermobile EDS. Also listed in the check log as the ScienceDirect KLK15 item; same paper.[20]
  • "Proteomic discoveries in hypermobile Ehlers-Danlos syndrome."[21]
  • "Label-free differentiation of classical and hypermobile Ehlers-Danlos syndromes using Mueller matrix polarimetry." Mueller matrix polarimetry distinguishing classical and hypermobile EDS.[22]
  • "Association between Ehlers-Danlos syndrome and complex regional pain syndrome: a large nationwide claims database study." Large nationwide claims database study.[23]
  • "Exploring cross-category relationships between symptoms in people with hypermobile EDS (hEDS) to identify disability patterns."[24]
  • "Obstructive sleep apnea and CPAP efficacy in patients with hypermobile Ehlers-Danlos syndrome and hypermobility spectrum disorder." Case-control study of obstructive sleep apnea and CPAP efficacy in hEDS and HSD.[25]
  • "Lower androgen sulfate metabolites in women with hypermobile Ehlers-Danlos syndrome."[26]
  • "Anesthesia Clinical Practice Guidelines for Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome: A Modified Delphi Survey." First consensus-based anesthesia practice guidelines for EDS.[27]
  • "A Major Update Is Coming to EDS and HSD Diagnosis." Updated EDS/HSD diagnostic criteria to be published in the American Journal of Medical Genetics in December 2026, with care and management pathways in March 2027.[28]
  • "New Model of Care for EDS and HSD." Model of Care work tied to Road to 2026.[29]
  • "Research Roundup: New Research in EDS and HSD." A roundup covering several studies, not a single article.[30]

Cranial cervical instability
  • "Evaluating the role of craniocervical and atlantoaxial instability in autonomic dysfunction: a narrative review." Narrative review of 43 studies.[31]
  • "Craniocervical instability after inadvertent neck hyperextension in Ehlers-Danlos syndrome: a retrospective case series and literature review." Eight hEDS patients developing CCI symptoms after procedures involving neck hyperextension.[32]
  • "Craniocervical instability in patients with Ehlers-Danlos syndromes: outcomes analysis following occipito-cervical fusion." Retrospective outcomes analysis of 53 EDS patients undergoing occipito-cervical fusion.[33]
  • "Craniocervical Instability in Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome, A Systematic Review of Diagnostic and Surgical Treatment Criteria." Systematic review.[34]
  • "Cervicogenic Dizziness Associated With Craniocervical Instability: A Case Report." Case report linking cervicogenic dizziness to CCI and clivo-axial angle correction.[35]
